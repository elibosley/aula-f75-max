Your devices

Fn+1, Fn+2 and Fn+3 switch Bluetooth devices. On Home, hold Fn and click the wheel to return to the previous Bluetooth device. Moving the physical selector takes priority.

Three saved Bluetooth pairings. The current firmware controls one link at a time; simultaneous-link switching is not implemented.

With the swap on, Caps sends Command on Mac and Ctrl on Windows or Android. Physical left Ctrl sends Caps Lock.

Your home screen

Accent colors adjust for readable text in each theme.

Clock uses this phone or computer’s local time. Sync again after a keyboard restart; time is not saved across power loss.

SMALL ROUTINES

Make a macro.

Choose a slot, add key presses and releases, then assign the slot to a key.

Primary means Command on Mac and Ctrl on Windows. Every press needs a release. Maximum 32 steps.

Editing never runs a macro. After applying, press its assigned physical key to test.

Apply changes the current session. Save also writes the keyboard’s flash, so settings survive a restart.
